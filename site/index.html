Costs on each day are for everyone there that day. Sunset is around 4:30pm in Amsterdam and 4:55pm in Paris, so outdoor things go mid-day and museums and lights go later.

Book these now

The trip is a little over two weeks out. Popular timeslots go first. Book everyone in one order so you get slots together. Ticks are saved in your own browser only, so they won't show up for anyone else.

    Good to know

    • ETIAS isn't required for this trip. It has no launch date yet, and there's a transition period once it starts.
    • Amsterdam: each person taps their own Visa or Mastercard (not Amex) in and out on trams and trains. One card can't pay for the group.
    • Paris: every person needs their own ticket, on a Navigo Easy card or on their phone. The nearest metro is Filles du Calvaire (line 8), about 4 minutes' walk from the Airbnb.
    • Sunset is around 4:30pm. Do outdoor stuff mid-day, museums and lights later.
    • Museums close one day a week. The Louvre and the Orangerie close Tuesdays. Orsay and the Musée Picasso close Mondays.
    • Splitting costs: one person books each group purchase and the others pay them back. A shared expense app (Splitwise or similar) keeps it simple.

    Assumptions

    • Flights, the Amsterdam hotel and the Paris Airbnb are already paid for and aren't counted.
    • 3 people in Amsterdam (Nov 28 – Dec 2) and on the Eurostar. 4 people in Paris (Dec 2 – 7). The 4th person's own travel to Paris isn't included.
    • The euro rate is €1 = $1.16.
    • Food is about €55 per person per day: bakery breakfast ~€7, casual lunch ~€18, sit-down dinner ~€25, coffee and snacks ~€5. Monday the 7th is breakfast only.
    • The Eurostar is estimated at €95 each for 3 seats bought now. Remove it if you've already booked.
    • Ticket prices are 2026 adult, non-EU prices from the sources below. Some differ between sources, so confirm when you book.
    • Card foreign-transaction fees can add 1–3% unless you use a no-FX-fee card.
