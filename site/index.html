    Good to know

    • ETIAS isn't required for this trip. It has no launch date yet, and there's a transition period once it starts.
    • Amsterdam: each person taps their own Visa or Mastercard (not Amex) in and out on trams and trains. One card can't pay for the group.
    • Paris: one Navigo Easy card each. The CDG airport ticket can't share a card with regular tickets, so buy it Monday morning.
    • Sunset is around 4:30pm. Do outdoor stuff mid-day, museums and lights later.
    • Lunch is easy to walk into, but book dinners on Fri and Sat, especially in Paris.
    • Splitting costs: one person books each group purchase and the others pay them back. A shared expense app (Splitwise or similar) keeps it simple.

    Assumptions

    • The euro rate is €1 = $1.16.
    • Flights: the first two seats are ≈$810 each, backed out of the original two-person budget. Each seat booked now is estimated at ≈$1,000 until it's actually booked.
    • Amsterdam hotel: $583 for one room for 4 nights. Rooms are doubles, and an odd person out turns one double into a triple (+$200).
    • Paris Airbnb: $700 for two, plus ≈$120 for each extra guest (fee and city tax). This assumes the place sleeps everyone.
    • Eurostar is estimated at €80 each. Being four weeks out and booking seats together pushes the typical €55–99 fare up.
    • Trip-wide extras are ≈$172 per person (the original $343 for two).
    • Food, local transit, museum and attraction tickets aren't included in the budget.
    • Card foreign-transaction fees can add 1–3% unless you use a no-FX-fee card.
