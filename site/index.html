    Good to know

    • ETIAS isn't required for this trip. It has no launch date yet, and there's a transition period once it starts.
    • Amsterdam: each person taps their own Visa or Mastercard (not Amex) in and out on trams and trains. One card can't pay for three people.
    • Paris: one Navigo Easy card each (€2). The CDG airport ticket can't share a card with regular tickets, so buy it Monday morning.
    • Sunset is around 4:30pm. Do outdoor stuff mid-day, museums and lights later.
    • Tables for three are easy at lunch but book dinners on Fri and Sat, especially in Paris.
    • Splitting costs: one person books each group purchase and the others pay them back. A shared expense app (Splitwise or similar) keeps it simple.
